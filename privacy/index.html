Privacy Notice

Workshop Hiring collects the information people choose to share with us so we can respond to hiring inquiries, review candidate interest, and route strong matches to the right hiring team.

Information We Collect

We may collect the information you submit through our forms, ads, email, or related hiring pages. That can include:

  • Name, email, phone number, and other contact details.
  • Role interests, work history, certifications, licensing status, languages, resume files, LinkedIn links, and other candidate materials.
  • Messages you send us about a role, company, school, or hiring need.
  • Basic source data such as referral page, campaign tags, and the form or page you used.

How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, review candidate fit for open roles, communicate with candidates, support hiring teams, maintain hiring records, improve our recruiting work, and prevent spam or misuse of our forms.

When We Share Information

We may share candidate information with the hiring organization connected to the role you expressed interest in. For Spark Academy recruiting, that means Workshop Hiring may route qualified candidate interest to Spark Academy or the Lawrence Public Schools hiring team.

We also use service providers for hosting, form processing, analytics, email, and related business operations. We do not sell personal information.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update, delete, or stop using your information by emailing mike@workshophiring.com. We may keep limited records when needed for legal, security, or hiring-process reasons.

We may update this notice as our hiring work changes. The effective date below shows when this version was published.
