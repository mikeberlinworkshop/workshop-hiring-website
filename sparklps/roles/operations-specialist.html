Background Information

Spark Academy in Lawrence, MA, is an innovative public school, part of Lawrence Public Schools, dedicated to creating a nurturing environment where every student thrives. Recognized among the top 4% of schools in the state as a “School of Recognition,” Spark Academy pairs research-driven academics, a focus on movement, and a strong, values-based culture to meet the needs of our students.

Behind every great school day is an operation that runs smoothly. The Operations Specialist keeps Spark's middle school humming — the front office, family communication, student records and logistics, and the dozens of details that let teachers teach and students learn. It is a fast, people-facing, highly organized role at the center of the building.

What you'll do

  • Run the front office and be a warm, organized first point of contact for students, families, and staff;
  • Communicate with families in both Spanish and English — by phone, in person, and in writing;
  • Keep student records, attendance, and enrollment information accurate and up to date;
  • Handle day-to-day logistics, scheduling, and supplies so the building runs smoothly;
  • Use school systems and software quickly and accurately, and process information fast;
  • Support school leaders on operations projects and other responsibilities as needed.

What we're looking for

  • Fluent in Spanish and English — you'll work directly with Spanish-speaking families (required);
  • Hyper-organized, efficient, and productive, with a positive, can-do attitude;
  • Comfortable with technology, and able to type and work quickly and accurately;
  • Solid with everyday, high-school-level math;
  • Evidence of hard work, reliability, and growing responsibility in past roles.
Position TypeFull-time
LocationSpark Academy · Lawrence, MA
LanguagesSpanish & English required
Starting DateSY 26-27

Job Requirements

  • High school diploma required; a college degree is not required.
  • No prior school-operations experience required — we will train the right person.
  • Citizenship, residency, or work visa required.
