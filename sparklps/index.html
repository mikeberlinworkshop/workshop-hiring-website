Our Story

A school on the move — literally.

About 13 years ago, Lawrence Public Schools entered receivership to turn around chronic underperformance. Spark Academy was founded in that moment of transformation. Within two years, we went from a Level 4 to a Level 1 school — the state's highest designation — and in 2023 we were named a School of Recognition for our achievement growth, a distinction earned by only 4% of schools in Massachusetts.

What makes Spark special? A positive, growth-oriented staff. A focus on movement. A deep commitment to research-driven practice. And, of course, our students.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Is Spark Academy a charter school?+
No. Spark Academy is a fully public school, part of Lawrence Public Schools. We do have “earned autonomy” — as a fully unionized public school, we keep some of the decision-making flexibility that helps us serve our students best.
Is there a teachers' union?+
Yes. As part of Lawrence Public Schools, Spark teachers and paraprofessionals can join their unions. The Lawrence Teachers and Paraprofessionals Unions offer strong benefits and advocate for the best possible learning community for LPS teachers, students, and families.
What support does Spark offer students?+
A safe, caring, academically rigorous environment. We have a fully staffed climate team supporting every grade level, plus school counselors available for students' social and emotional needs.
What other opportunities are there for students?+
About a third of our students take part in after-school programs — tutoring, clubs like theater and art, sports like basketball, soccer, and cheerleading, and advanced math. We also provide tailored learning intervention for students who need it.
What does “Active Living” mean here?+
It's one of our core values. Every student gets 50 minutes of fitness each day, plus 15 minutes of recess, and we encourage teachers to build movement into lessons. Our approach was featured on NPR's Only a Game.
